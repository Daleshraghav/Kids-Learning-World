📚 About Kids Learning World

Kids Learning World is a fun and interactive educational website created by Dalesh Raghav to make learning enjoyable for children.

Kids can learn the English ABC alphabet, Hindi alphabet, numbers and multiplication tables, days of the week, months of the year, fruits, animals, body parts and colours.

The goal of Kids Learning World is to provide simple, colourful and engaging learning activities that children can explore while learning.
